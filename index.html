About us

Games are what got me into computers. More than ten years of software development later, they still are.

Fernstalon Studios is my passion project: a way to give something back. It makes the kind of games I always wanted more of, for players who want them too, and for kids who might find in games a fun hobby that gives their brain a bit of a workout.
