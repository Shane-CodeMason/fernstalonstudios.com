The game

Heroes of legend against the horde

Every run starts with a choice of hero. You move, they fight: attacks fire on their own, so your job is to read the swarm, stay alive and pick the right upgrades as you level up.

Choose a hero, fight across five regions, pick up elements and upgrades as you go, and face the kings of each faction. Fall, and you come back with what you earned for the next attempt.

Twelve heroes

Each hero has their own weapon and their own attack.

Five regions

Meadow

Golden fields, old stone walls and a ruined castle.

Marsh

Stilt huts, weeping willows and dragonflies over the water.

Sanctuary

A ringed city of plazas, arches and towers.

Highlands

Terraced cliffs, cold lakes and a mountain to climb.

Caverns

A basalt pit of glowing crystals, mine carts and trestles.
